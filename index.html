01 — INTRODUCTION

Building solid foundations

DAC Group is a leading specialised Design & Build contractor, with a primary focus on state-of-the-art construction technologies. We provide quality services, delivered by a professional international team, associated with strong local presence.

We are differentiated by the quality of our people and our relentless drive to deliver the most successful outcomes — aligning our capabilities to our customers' objectives to create a lasting positive impact.

Vision, mission and values
